# Divine Life: daily flow

The home screen follows a single sequence: begin with Vinayagar, continue a
reading, notice today's practice, and take one small reflective action. The
opening is always the sourced `ganesha-aindhu-karathanai` text. It does not rotate
to another deity or prescribe a mandatory ritual count based on the hour.

The `(main)` Expo Router group owns four shared tabs: home, reading, mantras,
and conversation. Do not add a second row of shortcuts to the same destinations.
The reading hub contains Gita, Nayanmar lives, Purana introductions, source books,
and thematic reflection. Mantra browsing belongs in its own tab. Deity selection
is a preference available from the account/settings page, including for guests.

Deity artwork belongs in a focused devotional context, not behind every text
card. Home uses one contained Vinayagar image and plain, high-contrast surfaces.
Tamil text determines card height; avoid fixed text containers and clipped tab
labels. Readers retain their selectable reading palettes.

`Progress.readingLast` records the latest reading category independently from
`lastActivity` used by the conversation. Opening a mantra must not replace the
reading continuation. Home shows one reading first, with the rest behind an
explicit secondary control. Legacy saved positions remain available.

App-written reflection is presented as a small practical suggestion, not a
quotation, scripture, or divine promise. Source prayers and their explanations
remain separate in the mantra reader.
